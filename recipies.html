🍛 BIRYANI

🍛 HYDERABADI CHICKEN BIRYANI

Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice
  • Chicken
  • Onions
  • Yogurt
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • Mint and Coriander
  • Saffron
  • Traditional Biryani Spices

Preparation

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt and spices.
  2. Cook the basmati rice until partially done.
  3. Layer the marinated chicken and rice.
  4. Add fried onions, mint, coriander and saffron.
  5. Cook on low heat until the flavors blend together.
🍛 LUCKNOWI (AWADHI) BIRYANI

Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice
  • Chicken or Mutton
  • Yogurt
  • Onions
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • Whole Spices
  • Saffron

Preparation

  1. Marinate the meat with yogurt and spices.
  2. Cook the rice separately until partially done.
  3. Layer the meat and rice together.
  4. Add saffron and aromatic spices.
  5. Cook slowly until the rice becomes fragrant and fluffy.

🍩 CHURROS

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Water
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Cinnamon

Preparation

  1. Prepare the dough using flour, water and butter.
  2. Pipe the dough into long strips.
  3. Cook until golden and crispy.
  4. Coat with sugar and cinnamon.
  5. Serve with chocolate sauce.
